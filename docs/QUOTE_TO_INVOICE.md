# Quote to Invoice Conversion

QuoteMate streamlines your workflow by allowing you to convert accepted quotes directly into invoices, preserving all the details and saving time.

## Overview

When a customer accepts your quote, you can instantly convert it into an invoice with a single tap. All quote details are automatically transferred, so you can send an invoice immediately.

## Which Quotes Can Be Converted?

Quotes with the following statuses can be converted to invoices:

| Status | Can Convert |
|--------|-------------|
| `draft` | No |
| `sent` | Yes |
| `accepted` | Yes |
| `rejected` | No |

Typically, you'll convert quotes once they've been sent to and accepted by the customer.

## How to Convert a Quote

### Method 1: From the Quote View

1. Open the quote you want to convert
2. Tap the **Convert to Invoice** button
3. Review the pre-filled invoice details
4. Set payment terms (Due on Receipt, Net 7, 14, 30, or Custom)
5. Make any necessary adjustments
6. Save the invoice

### Method 2: From the Dashboard

1. Find the quote in your recent quotes list
2. Tap the overflow menu (three dots)
3. Select **Convert to Invoice**
4. Follow the same process as above

## What Gets Transferred

When converting a quote to an invoice, all relevant information is carried over:

### Customer Details
- Customer name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Address

### Job Information
- Job description
- Job type/template
- All notes

### Materials
- All materials with quantities
- Unit prices
- Material totals

### Pricing
- Labor hours and rate
- Markup percentage
- Subtotal
- GST calculation
- Total amount

## What's New on the Invoice

The converted invoice includes additional fields:

| Field | Default Value |
|-------|---------------|
| Invoice Number | Next sequential number |
| Invoice Date | Current date |
| Due Date | Calculated from payment terms |
| Payment Terms | Default or user-selected |
| Amount Paid | $0.00 |
| Status | `draft` |

## Adjusting the Invoice

After conversion, you can modify:

- **Payment Terms**: Change how long the customer has to pay
- **Materials**: Add, remove, or adjust quantities
- **Labor**: Update hours or rate
- **Markup**: Adjust markup percentage
- **Notes**: Add invoice-specific notes

## Original Quote Preservation

Converting a quote to an invoice:

- Does **not** delete the original quote
- Does **not** modify the original quote
- Creates a new, independent invoice document

You can still view, reference, and manage the original quote.

## Workflow Example

1. **Create Quote**: Build a detailed quote for a bathroom renovation
2. **Send Quote**: Share with customer via email
3. **Customer Accepts**: Quote status updated to `accepted`
4. **Convert to Invoice**: Create invoice from the accepted quote
5. **Set Terms**: Choose Net 14 payment terms
6. **Send Invoice**: Share invoice via email
7. **Record Payment**: Log payment when received

## Best Practices

### Before Converting

1. **Verify quote accuracy** - Ensure all materials and labor are correct
2. **Confirm acceptance** - Make sure the customer has formally accepted
3. **Check for updates** - Adjust if scope changed since quoting

### After Converting

1. **Review invoice** - Double-check all transferred details
2. **Set appropriate terms** - Choose payment terms based on your agreement
3. **Send promptly** - Invoice while the work is fresh
4. **Track payments** - Monitor for timely payment

## Multiple Invoices from One Quote

In some scenarios, you might need to create multiple invoices from a single quote:

- **Progress billing**: Invoice at project milestones
- **Split payments**: Separate invoices for deposit and balance
- **Change orders**: Additional work beyond original scope

To create additional invoices:

1. Convert the quote as normal
2. Adjust the invoice amount for the specific portion
3. Repeat for additional invoices as needed

Note: Each conversion creates a full invoice that can be edited to reflect partial amounts.

## Quote and Invoice Relationship

While invoices can be created from quotes, they become independent documents:

- Editing a quote does not affect converted invoices
- Editing an invoice does not affect the source quote
- Both documents maintain their own history and status

## Tips for Efficient Conversion

1. **Use consistent quote details** - Accurate quotes mean accurate invoices
2. **Keep quotes updated** - Ensure pricing reflects current rates
3. **Set default payment terms** - Configure your preferred terms in Settings
4. **Convert promptly** - Don't delay invoicing after acceptance
