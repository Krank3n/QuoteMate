# Why QuoteMate is the Best Tradie App in Australia

## Fastest Quoting in the Game

- **Describe your job, get a materials list** — tell QuoteMate what the job is and it suggests materials with quantities. No more guessing or Googling.
- **Section-based quoting** — quote the way you actually work (e.g. "8 fence bays, 2 gates") with per-section labor and materials.
- **Reusable job templates** — save your common jobs (deck, bathroom reno, rewire) and create quotes in minutes, not hours.
- **Voice-to-text job descriptions** — talk into your phone on-site, QuoteMate writes the job description.
- **Quote to invoice in one tap** — accepted quote becomes an invoice instantly, no re-entering anything.

## Live Supplier Pricing No Other App Has

- **Bunnings pricing** — real-time prices pulled directly from Bunnings.
- **Reece API integration** — live trade pricing for plumbers, straight from Reece.
- **Web search pricing** — finds prices from any supplier across the web when Bunnings/Reece don't stock it.
- **Supplier list importer** — snap a photo of a supplier price list or PDF and it extracts every line item automatically.
- **Personal supplier rates** — save your trade pricing so your quotes always use your actual costs, not retail.
- **Favourites system** — save your go-to products with your prices, coverage rates, and notes.
