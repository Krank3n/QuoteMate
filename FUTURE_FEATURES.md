# QuoteMate — Future Feature Ideas

Killer features to differentiate in the AU/NZ trades market.

## Pricing & Quoting

- **Live supplier price sync** — Real-time pricing across Bunnings/Reece/others, auto-pick cheapest. Tradies currently check 3-4 supplier sites manually.
- **Regional price adjustment** — Auto-adjust estimates based on postcode (metro Sydney vs rural QLD varies massively).
- **Quote confidence scoring** — "Based on 200 similar fencing jobs in VIC, your quote is 12% below average." Helps tradies stop undercharging.

## Winning More Work

- **Customer-facing quote portal** — Branded web link instead of PDF email. Customer can accept, ask questions, request changes. Track when they open it.
- **Smart follow-up nudges** — "This quote was opened 3 times but not accepted — send a follow-up?" Most tradies lose jobs by forgetting to chase.
- **Photo-to-scope** — Tradie takes photos on site, app generates draft material list and measurements from images.

## Job Intelligence

- **Material waste calculator** — Auto-add industry-standard waste percentages (10% timber, 15% tiles, etc.).
- **Seasonal demand alerts** — "Deck builds spike in October in your area — consider raising your labour rate." Based on aggregated app data.
- **Supplier delivery scheduling** — Coordinate material delivery with job start date. NZ lead times especially are a nightmare.

## Tradie-Specific Pain Points Nobody Solves

- **Scope creep tracker** — Customer asks for extras on-site, tradie taps "add variation," generates mini-quote signed on the phone. Protects against "but I thought that was included."
- **Subcontractor quoting** — Forward part of a job (electrical, plumbing) to a subbie who quotes within the app, rolls into main quote automatically.
- **QBCC / licensed work compliance** — Auto-flag when job value crosses thresholds requiring written contracts (QLD: $3,300+). Different rules per state.
- **Weather-aware scheduling** — Pull BOM forecasts, warn when outdoor jobs scheduled during rain.

## The Real Moat

**Network effects from supplier discovery.** If tradies can find and compare suppliers through the app, and suppliers can list/promote themselves, QuoteMate becomes a marketplace — not just a tool. Exponentially harder to compete with.

## Priority Picks

Highest impact for lowest build cost:

1. **Quote portal with open-tracking**
2. **Variation / scope-creep tool**
3. **Material waste calculator**

Daily pain, not complex to build, nothing in the AU/NZ market (Tradify, ServiceM8, Fergus, Buildxact) does them well for small operators.
